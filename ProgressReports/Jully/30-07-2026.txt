🔔 3. Notifications Refactoring & Fixes

To resolve constructor parameter mismatch alerts (PHP0443: Too many arguments, P1119), all Notification classes were refactored with typed constructors:

AccountDeactivatedNotification: Accepts ?string $reason

WelcomeNotification: Parameterless instantiation

NewMessageNotification: Accepts Message $message

RatingSubmittedNotification: Accepts Rating $rating

RatingRepliedNotification: Accepts RatingReply $reply

ReportSubmittedNotification: Accepts Report $report

ReportStatusChangedNotification: Accepts Report $report, string $oldStatus, string $newStatus

ReportRepliedNotification: Accepts ReportReply $reply
